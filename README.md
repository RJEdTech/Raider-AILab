# Raider AILab

How large language models work, why their training goals shape what they do, where AI helps and where it hurts, and what it costs. Interactive tools for Regis Jesuit students, built to demystify rather than scare. Part of the Raider Labs suite.

**Live site:** https://rjedtech.github.io/Raider-AILab/

## Sections
- **How a language model works:** build a tiny next-word model and turn the temperature dial.
- **Goals shape behavior:** grade a bot that learns to guess, train a machine that learns to flatter, watch an agent game its goal, and rewrite an AI's written priorities.
- **Working with AI:** the jagged frontier, learning with AI versus from it, and what an AI-writing flag really means.
- **What AI costs:** energy, water and carbon per prompt, scaled up, with the counting choices made visible.

Every figure in a tool links to its source (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, IEA, LBNL, peer-reviewed studies), with dates.

## Files
`index.html`, `engine.js`, `biolab.css`, `courses.js`, `sims/*.js`, `practical/*.js`, `review/*.js`. Everything runs in the browser; no student data leaves the device.
